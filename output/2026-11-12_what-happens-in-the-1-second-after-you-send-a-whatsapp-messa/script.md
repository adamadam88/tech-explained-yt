# What Happens the Second You Hit Send on WhatsApp 💬

**Topic:** What happens in the 1 second after you send a WhatsApp message?  
**Category:** internet  
**Voice:** am_michael @ 1.0

| # | t | visual | narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0s | hook | Your WhatsApp message is **locked** before it even leaves your phone. |
| 2 | 3.5s | insight | It's called end-to-end encryption: your phone locks it with a key only your friend's phone has. |
| 3 | 9.6s | flow | Then, in a fraction of a second, it hops to a cell tower, races through fiber cables, and reaches WhatsApp's servers. |
| 4 | 17.3s | versus | But those servers only ever see **scrambled noise**. Not even WhatsApp itself can read what you wrote. |
| 5 | 23.6s | list | One grey tick: the server has it. Two ticks: it reached their phone. Blue: they **read** it. |
| 6 | 28.8s | number | And WhatsApp does this about **100 billion** times a day, for over two billion people. |
| 7 | 34.5s | reveal | And every **single** one? |
